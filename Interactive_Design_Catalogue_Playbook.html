01 — Vision

From static catalogue to a live brand experience.

Every new Vantage Circle client onboards with their own whitelabel — logo, colors, identity. The catalogue should match.

Replace the static PDF catalogue with an interactive page where a client drops in their logo and brand colors and instantly sees every Vantage Circle collateral — badges, banners, certificates — rendered in their brand. Sales closes faster. Design builds less custom work. Onboarding goes from days to minutes.
02 — Problem

The catalogue can't show what clients actually want to see.

When a prospective client asks for samples, we send a static PDF of generic, non-branded designs. They can't visualize their own logo and colors on it — so the design team produces one-off custom mockups just to close the deal.

Current Pain

  • Static PDFs show generic badges that look nothing like the client's eventual branding
  • Every prospect requests "can I see this with our logo?" — design team builds bespoke mockups
  • Back-and-forth revisions cost design hours that should be spent on production work
  • Sales/onboarding loses momentum waiting for custom samples between meetings
  • No way to preview a badge inside the actual Vantage Circle feed or login screen
  • Clients ask for unrealistic edits because they can't see the template's constraints
  • Approvals get stuck — HR head can't share the PDF with their CMO for sign-off in a useful form

What Changes

  • One link opens an interactive catalogue with the client's logo and color already applied
  • Every badge, banner and certificate previews live, in-context, inside mock Vantage Circle screens
  • Design team approves templates once; the app applies branding automatically forever after
  • Custom mockups for sales drop sharply — most clients self-select what they want
  • Clients can save, compare, and share their selections for internal approval
  • Submissions arrive structured: logo file, hex codes, template IDs, all variable text
03 — Solution

An interactive catalogue powered by a brand-first flow.

The app captures brand inputs once, then renders every collateral in the client's identity. A persistent preview panel and platform-context mockups close the gap between "sample" and "what I'll actually get."

A

Brand Setup First

Logo upload, primary & secondary colors (auto-extracted from logo), company name, tagline. Every later screen uses this context.

B

Asset-Specific Templates

Monetary badges, non-monetary badges, LSA tiers, certificates, feed banners, login banners — each with their own variable fields and constraints.

C

In-Platform Previews

Badges shown inside a mock feed post. Login banners shown with the login card overlaid. Certificates shown in a frame mockup. No more isolated assets.

D

Structured Submission

Selections route to design team with logo file, exact hex codes, template ID, variable text and asset specs — no re-derivation needed.

05 — Features

What the app does, end to end.

Built around one truth: the client should never see a collateral that isn't already in their brand.

01

Brand Capture & Auto-Palette

Upload logo (PNG/SVG). Dominant colors are extracted automatically. Client confirms or overrides primary/secondary. WCAG contrast is validated.

02

Live Template Renderer

SVG-based templates with logo, color, and text slots. Every change updates previews in real time across all selected assets.

03

Asset Library

Monetary Badge, Non-Monetary Badge, LSA Badge (with year tiers), Certificate, Feed Banner, Login Banner. Multiple style variants per type.

04

In-Platform Context Previews

Badges rendered inside a mock recognition feed. Banners shown above a mock login. Certificates shown in award-frame context.

05

Compare & Save

Side-by-side comparison of variants. Save sessions by magic-link. Share with internal stakeholders. Comment per asset.

06

Brand Kit Export

Generate a single PDF preview of all selected assets in the client's brand — shareable for internal approval before submission.

07

Design Team Authoring

Designers add new templates without engineering involvement — mark logo/color/text slots in a template authoring view.

08

Structured Handoff

Submissions arrive in a queue with logo file, exact hex codes, template IDs, variable text, and per-asset comments — ready for production.

09

Status Tracking

Client sees real-time progress: In queue → In design → Ready for review → Delivered. Eliminates "where is it?" emails.

10

Approval & Revision Loop

Designer delivers v1 in-app. Client approves or comments per asset. Versions are preserved.

11

Brand Profiles

Saved per client. Six months later, the client adds a new award category — brand is already loaded.

12

Internal Analytics

Most-picked templates, dead-weight options, asset bundles. Tells design what to expand and what to retire.

08 — UX problems, fixed

What the static PDF gets wrong — and what the app fixes.

Side-by-side audit of the experience today versus what changes when the catalogue is interactive.

UX Topic
Static PDF — Problem
Interactive App — Fix
Brand visualization
Client sees generic, off-brand samples — has to mentally project their logo onto someone else's design.
Logo and colors applied live to every preview before the client clicks anything.
Context
Badges and banners shown floating on blank backgrounds — no sense of how they appear in real use.
Every asset shown inside a mock Vantage Circle screen — feed post, login page, certificate frame.
Selection workflow
No way to mark, save, or share what they like — replies arrive as "page 4, second badge, but green."
Click to select. Compare options side-by-side. Save sessions and share via link.
Stakeholder approval
Client forwards a PDF to their CMO who has no context and replies "looks fine, I guess."
Shareable link shows everything in the client's brand. Comments per asset. Real internal sign-off.

Grouped by use case ("Recognition," "Platform," "Awards") so clients pick a bundle, not a guess.

Editable text fields render live. Long names, short names — they see exactly how it lays out.
Color safety

WCAG contrast checked live. Client warned if combination fails. Brand-safe themes only.

Submissions arrive structured: template IDs, hex codes, logo file, comments. Production-ready.

Live status: In queue → In design → Ready for review → Delivered. No more chase emails.
