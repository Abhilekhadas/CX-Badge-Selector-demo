Simple Steps

How it works

From your company name to a confirmed design — in five guided steps.

  1. Add your company name

    Every preview that follows picks it up automatically.

  2. Pick your bundles

    Choose the collateral you need — awards, banners, mailers, and more.

  3. Choose a design style

    Minimal, Premium, or Fun & Colourful — one per bundle.

  4. Select your badges

    Pick from 30 ready-made designs in your chosen style.

  5. Customise & confirm

    Tweak names and colours with a live preview, then confirm.

The eight bundles

What's inside the catalogue

Each bundle covers one part of the employee experience — pick the ones your program needs.

Non-Monetary Awards

Recognition badges without a cash value — peer shout-outs and value-based callouts.

Monetary Awards

Reward badges that carry points to spend in the Vantage rewards store.

Long Service Awards

Tiered milestone badges for 1, 3, 5, 10, 20, and 25 years of service.

Feed Banner

A wide image at the top of the in-app recognition feed for campaigns and events.

Certificate

A printable, shareable award certificate with a framed layout.

Teaser

A pre-launch banner that builds curiosity before the program goes live.

Launch Mailer

A branded HTML email that introduces the program on day one.

Login Banner

The branded visual above the sign-in card for non-SSO employees.
